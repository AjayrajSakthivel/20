Section 01 — Philosophy

Most institutes sell a course.
Beyond20 sells an outcome.

Anyone can teach you what a dropdown menu in Oracle Fusion does, or how to configure a pricing procedure in SAP SD. That's the easy part — and it's the part every institute in Bangalore already claims to offer.

The harder, more valuable part is what happens after: can you explain your project in an interview under pressure? Can you answer a curveball technical question without freezing? Do you know how to position four years of unrelated work experience as a strength instead of a gap?

That's the actual skill gap between "trained" and "hired" — and it's the gap Beyond20 was built to close.
